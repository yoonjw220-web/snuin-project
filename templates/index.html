                        How your estimate is worked out
                          Early payment

                          You can take part of your expected payment before the credits sell. The amount taken early is deducted from your final payment, along with a fixed service charge shown before you agree. If the credits sell for less than expected, you keep what you were already paid — we carry that risk, not you.

                          What is not guaranteed
                          • Carbon income is not a guaranteed payment.
                          • An independent verifier may reduce or reject the claimed amount.
                          • Credit prices move, so the final figure can differ from the estimate.
                          • Weather or a poor harvest can reduce how much straw you have.
                          Leaving, and complaints

                          You can leave at any time before the verification package is submitted, at no cost. After submission you can still leave, but credits already issued for that season stay with the project. To complain, speak to your local CarbonBridge field officer, or contact the farmer representative on the programme committee. If it is still unresolved, it goes to the independent grievance line printed on your contract.

                          Your data

                          Your photos, weights and field location belong to you. You can ask for a copy or ask us to delete them. Withdrawing consent removes your field from the project. Buyers only ever see totals, never your name or your photos.

                          Take part of your payment early

                          Your credits have not sold yet. You can take part of what you expect to earn now, and it is deducted when the sale completes.
